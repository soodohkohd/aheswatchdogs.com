Who are Watch D.O.G.S.®

Fathers, mothers, grandparents, stepparents, aunts, uncles, guardians, and other caring adults serve on campus each year under the leadership of a program coordinator and the guidance of the school principal.

Purpose

There are two primary goals of the Watch D.O.G.S. program:

  1. To provide positive role models for the students, demonstrating by their presence that education is important.
  2. To provide extra sets of eyes and ears to enhance school security and safety.

At our school, we believe that families and educators share common values and a collective desire to see every child thrive. The Watch D.O.G.S. program helps bring our community closer by uniting us around these shared goals — supporting our educators and inspiring our students, one caring adult at a time.

Getting started is easy

Register

Fill out the Watch D.O.G.S. registration form online.

Join the PTA®

The program is part of the AHES PTA, so all volunteers register with the PTA.

Inspire the students

Complete two short training videos, then serve on campus as a positive role model.
